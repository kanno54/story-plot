浴衣連作プロンプト集（6枚）

使い方メモ
- 各番号ごとに、そのまま1本ずつコピペして使う想定です。
- 6枚とも、共通キャラ設定・共通作画ルールを本文に統合済みです。
- 吹き出しは日本語で指定しています。
- 連作として統一したいので、毎回「same character design throughout the series」を入れています。

==================================================
1. 試着室：「似合う」と言われ慣れてない
==================================================
masterpiece, best quality, anime illustration, clean detailed lineart, slightly painterly cel shading, cinematic warm indoor lighting, vertical 4:5 composition, comic illustration style with speech bubbles, polished single-panel manga illustration, same character design throughout the whole six-image series.

Two adult Japanese men.
Character A: an adult Japanese man in his late twenties, very tall and broad, strongly built with a heavy athletic muscular physique, thick neck, broad shoulders, solid chest, powerful arms, large hands, sturdy masculine frame, clearly big and strong but not exaggerated like a bodybuilder, rugged handsome face, short black hair with a slightly spiky natural texture, very thick dark eyebrows, narrow dark eyes, strong jawline, straight nose, light blush when embarrassed, stern expression that becomes shy when flustered, clean-shaven. He wears a dark navy yukata with a subtle woven geometric pattern, a muted blue-gray obi, and wooden geta.
Character B: an adult Japanese man in his mid to late twenties, slightly shorter than Character A, lean and toned rather than bulky, graceful but masculine build, calm intelligent face, black hair with soft layered bangs, expressive dark eyes, refined features, gentle but teasing expression, mature and attractive, not childlike, clean-shaven. He wears a dark brown or charcoal-brown striped yukata with a dark obi and simple geta.
Relationship and mood: they are longtime childhood friends with quiet romantic tension. Character B is more openly teasing and caring. Character A is gruff, easily embarrassed, and blushes when praised or taken care of.

Scene: inside a traditional yukata shop at night, warm indoor lighting, shelves of folded yukata fabrics in the background, a fitting curtain partly open. Character A stands in front of the fitting space, awkward and visibly embarrassed in his dark navy yukata, one hand touching the back of his neck. Character B stands in front of him in his dark brown striped yukata, looking up at him with surprise and admiration. This is the moment Character B realizes just how good Character A looks in yukata.

Composition: full upper-to-lower body view of Character A, Character B visible from the back and side, both clearly readable. Focus on Character A’s broad frame in the yukata and his blushing face.

Mood: shy, intimate, slightly comedic, warm.

Include clean white Japanese speech bubbles with black outline and legible vertical Japanese text. Include these Japanese speech bubbles:
- Character A: 「こんなの俺には似合わねえって」
- Character B: 「……何だよ、その反応」
- Character B: 「いや、めちゃくちゃ似合ってる」
- Character A: 「……そういうの、いいから」

Constraints: keep both characters clearly adult and mature-looking. Keep Character B mature and attractive, not childlike. Preserve the same face shape, eyebrows, hairstyle, and age impression throughout the series. No extra limbs, no duplicated hands, no malformed fingers, no text outside speech bubbles, no chibi style, no overly realistic photo style.
